About

I'm Derosier Overlin, a computer science and cybersecurity student at Université Roi Henri Christophe (URHC) in Cap-Haïtien, Haiti. I do independent security research under the name D3BUG.REG — mapping attack surfaces, tracing auth flows, and reporting what I find through proper channels.

This blog is my public notebook: methodology, tooling, and lessons from real engagements. No exploit dumps, no undisclosed vulnerabilities.

Disclosure policy

  1. Report privately first. Findings go to the vendor or the bug bounty program before anywhere else.
  2. Give them time. I follow the program's disclosure timeline and default to patience over publicity.
  3. Publish only after the fix — or with explicit permission. This blog documents how I work, not live holes in live systems.

Rules of engagement

  • Authorized targets and own accounts only.
  • Passive recon and targeted manual requests, at a human pace.
  • No brute force, no DoS, no spam, no phishing, no automated scanners against live targets.

Contact

For security reports, use the vendor's official channel or bug bounty program — not this blog. For everything else, reach me via GitHub.
